Phuentsholing is a border town in the south of Bhutan, sitting right next to the Indian town of Jaigaon. Because of its location it is busy with trade, and trucks carrying goods move through the town gate almost all day. The weather is warm and humid for most of the year, which is very different from the cold valleys further north. During the monsoon the rain can be heavy for days at a time, and the hills behind the town turn a deep green.

The College of Science and Technology sits on a hill above the town. Students come from every district of the country to study engineering, architecture and information technology. A normal day starts with morning classes, followed by labs in the afternoon. In the evenings the hostels are noisy with people finishing assignments, cooking instant noodles and arguing about football. Exam weeks are quieter, and the library stays full until late at night.

Studying algorithms is a big part of the software engineering programme. At first the topics can feel abstract, because sorting a list or finding a shortest path seems simple when the input is small. The real lesson appears when the input grows. An algorithm that takes a fraction of a second for a thousand items might need hours for a million items if its running time grows with the square of the input size. Learning to predict this growth before writing any code is one of the most useful skills a programmer can have.

Compression is a good example of an algorithmic idea that people use every day without noticing. Text, images and videos are all compressed before they are stored or sent over the internet. Huffman coding is one of the oldest and simplest methods. It gives short codes to characters that appear often and longer codes to characters that are rare. In normal English text the space and the letter e are very common, while letters like q, z and x are rare, so the savings can be quite large. The method is greedy: at every step it joins the two least frequent groups, and it can be proven that this choice always leads to an optimal prefix code.

After a long week, many students walk down to the river or take a bus up the winding road towards Thimphu for the weekend. The road climbs through thick forest and clouds, and the temperature drops quickly. By the time the bus reaches the top of the pass, the warm air of the border town feels far away.
